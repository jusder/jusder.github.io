In the works

Your day,
kept thoughtfully.

DayWise is a daily journal for the moments worth remembering — moods, places, photos, small wins, hard days. Quiet on the page, ready when you are.

Coming soon iOS macOS iCloud sync

What it does

✏

Write the way you think.

Rich text editor with markdown shortcuts, slash commands, interactive checklists, and a template library for the prompts you come back to.

💓

Mood & emotion tracking.

A simple 1–10 mood with a tiered emotion picker. Wellbeing surfaces patterns over time — without judgment.

🌍

Context that matters.

Weather, location, calendar events, and photos quietly attach so each entry remembers its day. Set a place as Home; we'll use that name.

🔒

Yours, privately.

Entries live locally and sync across your devices via your own iCloud — encrypted, no DayWise server in the middle.

DayWise is still being made. The shape is settling in (rich text editor, mood & emotion tracking, photo embedding, CloudKit sync, on-this-day, tags, templates), but it isn't on the App Store yet. Check back, or keep an eye on the homepage for when it ships.
